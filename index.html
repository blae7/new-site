Deep Time

4.6 billion years of Earth's history, from molten rock to right now — scroll through it, and at any point in time, invent a character of your own and watch an imagined ripple spread forward through everything after it.

The eras and dates are real. The ripples your characters cause are generative fiction, built fresh in your browser — a playful "what if," not a science claim.

The story is yours from here.
